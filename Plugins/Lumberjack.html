What Is Lumberjack?

Lumberjack adds tree gravity and fast leaf decay. Tree gravity only works on natural logs, so your builds are not at risk. Tree gravity also does not break all logs at once, rather causes them to fall like sand.


How Do We Use Lumberjack?

We use tree gravity to make it easier to collect wood and to add a fun extra feature to the server. It is optional to use.
