What Is BlueMap?

BlueMap creates an automatically updating map of the server online. It has different views such as spectator, flat, and surface. It also shows where players are and other custom markers.

How Do We Use BlueMap?

We use BlueMap as an easy way to view the server. We also use it to mark where players have their bases saved and what chunks a nation owns. Out map is available here!
