What Is Graves?

As the name suggests, when you die a grave spawns. It contains your items, experience, and an obituary how you died. There are numerous options to customize the plugin.


How Do We Use Graves?

We use the graves to get rid of the annoyance of getting killed and looted. We have a protection time on the graves where only you can access it, but after that it's fair game. There's also the chance to get your player head from a grave, which is a fun decoration - or war trophy.
