What Is DiscordSRV?

DiscordSRV allows servers to link the chat and the console to Discord channels. It also allows players to link their Discord and Minecraft accounts together.


How Do We Use DiscordSRV?

We use it to link the Minecraft chat with Discord and to remotely monitor the console for suspicious activity.
