What Is UberEnchant?

Using commands, enchantments are capped at 255 instead of their normal levels, and you can add any enchantment to any item.

How Do We Use UberEnchant?

We use it to enchant previously enchanted items with higher levels. Even if an item has, for example, Sharpness III, the vanilla enchant command won't apply Sharpness IV, so this allows the bot to do that.
