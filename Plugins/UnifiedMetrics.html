What Is UnifiedMetrics?

UnifiedMetrics collects information on a server and exports it to a data manager called Prometheus. Using another service called Grafana, the datasources can be queried for their info and displayed on a dashboard.

How Do We Use UnifiedMetrics?

We use UnifiedMetrics to make a cool stats monitoring page. It has our uptime, player/mob counts, message count, hardware utililization, and more.
