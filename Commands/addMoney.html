/addMoney

Description

Adds money to a player's bank. Used when there was a bug and either too much money was taken, or not enough given.

Permission

Economy Admin

Syntax

/addMoney <player> <amount>

Arguments

player is the player to receive the money. Required.

amount is the amount of money to give the player. Must be larger than 0. Required.

Examples

Adding Money To A Player

Related Commands

forceClockOut removeMoney setMoney

Invalid Conditions

Negative amount provided.

Example - Adding Money To A Player

/addMoney SomeMineGame 300.75

Result

SomeMineGame will gain $300.75 to his bank account.
