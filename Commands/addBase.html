/addBase

Description

Saves your base for future access. Both you and other players can view it with the bot. An icon will be made on the 3D map.

Permission

Everyone

Syntax

/addBase <x> <y> <z>

Arguments

x is your base's x coordinate. Required.

y is your base's y coordinate. Required.

z is your base's z coordinate. Required.

Examples

Add Base

Related Commands

editBase removeBase viewBase

Invalid Conditions

You already have a base saved.

Provided coordinates are outside build area.

Example - Adding A Base

/addBase 100 68 -125

Result

This player's base will be saved at the coordinates of 100 68 -125.
