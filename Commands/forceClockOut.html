/forceClockOut

Description

Forcefully clocks a user out of work. Used when a player doesn't clock out within a reasonable amount of time after leaving the server or going AFK. An autoclockout feature is enabled, but due to connection issues may fail.

Permission

Economy Admin

Syntax

/forceClockOut <player> <keep>

Arguments

player is the player to be clocked out. Required.

keep is whether or not the player should keep their earnings. A general rule of thumb for keeping is a maximum of 5% of their playtime. Required.

Examples

Keeping Earnings Removing Earnings

Related Commands

addMoney clockIn clockOut removeMoney setMoney

Invalid Conditions

Player is already clocked out.

Example - Keeping Earnings

/forceClockOut SomeMineGame True

Result

SomeMineGame will be clocked out and paid normally.

Example - Removing Earnings

/forceClockOut SomeMineGame False

Result

SomeMineGame will be clocked out and lose the earnings from the session.
