Legacy Of Lexicus Partnership

Hello, SomeMineGame here. Last week I made a development post about the current state of the project. There has since been a new update! I have decided to enter a partnership with my friend Skittally to keep development of the server progressing. This means I am no longer the sole person working on the project. I am still in the process of setting up all the development environments and plans, so it may still be a while for updates to resume, but they will be on the way!

This partnership involves assistance with the bot development, Minecraft server management, and some wiki updating for the time being. I will still be working on the wiki's main development. We also will make most non-financial related decisions together, so only high quality and high priority changes (as opposed to my minor changes every hour 😅) will happen. If we turn a profit off Patreon Members, there will be no change to the percentage going towards charity or the project's savings, it will cut into my share :).

Skittally has a history of Python programming as well as lots free time, which made them my go to candidate. They have also offered to help in the past, so I knew they would love the opportunity to join the project. Skittally, welcome to the team!
